The two Poly Haven quiver trees are imported comparison candidates. They are
not integrated into the world or claimed visually accepted. The publisher's
common names are retained; no Latin species is asserted for these assets.
Their presence here does not change the contents of an earlier player package.

R05 PH01 derivative experiment: the downloaded FBX/textures remain unchanged,
but five extracted groups of 21 source leaves are placed on modified procedural
architecture. Source UVs/normals are retained with coordinate and pivot changes;
generated materials use a modest leaf tint and interior source-atlas bark regions.
Separate unidentified basal pieces are diagnostic-only (18-21), not part of the
hybrid trees. This derivative is distinct from R03's original-model comparison;
no accepted world integration or fresh full numeric/bake/LOD validation is claimed.


Editor-generated PH02 crown derivative trial (2026-09-10): source downloads stay
unchanged. The explicit 0.65m horizontal cut preserves original vertex normals
and polygon-corner UVs, interpolating boundary attributes in metre/Y-up output.
The boundary stays open; no cap/socket/weld is added. A separate gray open
support tests a translated attachment, without changing the source crown scale.
Full/cut/attached comparison is unaccepted; complete leaves and fit need actual
3D review. Source taxonomy remains publisher-labelled quiver tree only.
