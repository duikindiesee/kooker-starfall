R05 derivative use (2026-09-10): the downloaded files stay unchanged. Five
source rosette groups of 21 leaf components are extracted, converted around
root pivots, and placed on modified procedural architecture. Source UVs and
normals are retained; generated leaf materials have a modest colour tint.
Generated bark maps use interior rectangles of the source UV atlas. This is
a modified inspection asset, separate from the R03 original-model comparison.
Nearby closed basal components remain unidentified and are used only in
separate diagnostics 18-21, not in the hybrid trees. Full rendering, fresh
numeric/bake/LOD validation and visual acceptance are not established here.
