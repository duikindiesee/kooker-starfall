# Starfall plant direction and retained ideas

Decision recorded 14 September 2026. This is an approved design direction, not
evidence of a new compiled or accepted plant asset.

## First forage plant: approved for implementation

Replace the smooth teal canopy discs with an original sourfig-inspired succulent.
Use low, irregular branching growth, thick tapered fleshy leaves, pink-purple
flowers and separate readable ripe fruit. Fruit should grow within the plant,
not hang beneath umbrella-like discs. Keep sandy gaps and a grounded woody base;
avoid floating stems, buried foliage, and clipping against rocks.

Keep the existing deterministic gather/eat/seed behavior and stable food identity.
If presentation names change, map them explicitly; do not silently migrate saves
or create a second food resource. Decorative flowers are not automatically edible.
This fictional plant is not a real-world foraging guide.

Acceptance needs an actual-player near view, normal gameplay-distance view,
camera orbit, grounded placement, visible ripe/depleted states, and a successful
gather/eat interaction. Concept art and source checks do not satisfy those gates.

## Inspiration and alternatives retained for later

- Age of Empires-like readability: a compact resource silhouette that clearly
  communicates something to gather; use original artwork, not extracted assets.
- Desert cactus-like fruit plants: thick pads or lobes with distinct fruit.
- Cape coastal berry shrubs: dense irregular foliage over branching woody stems.
- Namaqualand/Succulent Karoo rocky pockets: fleshy plants, open sand, seasonal
  flower colour; fynbos is an additional influence, not the same biome.
- Some fictional flowers or other plant parts could become food only through
  explicit rules and grounded discovery. Appearance alone must not teach safety.
- Future ecology: finite fruit, regrowth, seed drops with lifetimes, occasional
  natural germination, suitable soil, learned planting and maintenance, scarcity
  and difficulty. These are retained ideas, not added requirements for this
  visual replacement or claims of implemented ecology.

## Primary research references

- [SANBI: Carpobrotus deliciosus](https://pza.sanbi.org/carpobrotus-deliciosus):
  spreading succulent foliage, purple-pink flowers and fruit inspiration.
- [SANBI: Carpobrotus mellei](https://pza.sanbi.org/carpobrotus-mellei):
  dry fynbos and Succulent Karoo context.
- [SANBI: Searsia glauca](https://pza.sanbi.org/searsia-glauca):
  coastal shrub and berry silhouette inspiration.
- [SANBI: Succulent Karoo](https://pza.sanbi.org/vegetation/succulent-karoo-biome):
  regional vegetation context.

References inform original art; linked photographs are not automatically licensed
for import. Retain concept art separately from compiled-player evidence, with
source/build provenance and a truthful Discord-ready narrative. External posting
remains subject to the publication workflow.
