| Stage | Scope | Required gate | Current status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 — Living thought, refuge, berry | Safe lesson, finite food/water, bounded Eden, save, visible decisions | Component player then integrated player/input acceptance | v0.1.2 scripted loop/Eden/isolation and v0.1.5 Editor native visual/input passed; standalone signing and integration open |

| 2 — Predator perception and escape | Bounded sensing/pursuit, threat cues, retreat | Notice danger and escape without magical knowledge | Planned |
| 3 — Shelter threat | Refuge approach, breaches and exposure | Readable limits, recovery and fair counterplay | Planned |
| 4 — Learned nonlethal deterrent | Taught noise or barrier mechanic | Evidence-linked learning, costs/cooldown, bounded animal response | Planned |
| 5 — Defensive crafting/combat | Later fire/barriers/stakes or restrained defense | Earlier stages accepted; ingredient/permission rules, humane presentation, ecological limits | Planned; no hunting/combat in food fixture |

Every stage separates design, source implementation, automated evidence, rendered runtime, input-device checks and human acceptance. Do not import roadmap systems into the active combined-world checkout merely because they appear here. Integration needs a reviewed adapter and its own evidence.

Body-shape changes, clinical nutrition, general farming and open-ended predators are outside this slice. Rates are fictional tuning, deliberately compressed or generous as labelled.
