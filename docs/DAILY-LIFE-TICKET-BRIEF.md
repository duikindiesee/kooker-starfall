## Gameplay rationale

Give the inhabitant meaningful choices rather than constant chores. A baseline
day should allow discovery, replenishment, useful work and rest, with slack for
conversation and unexpected changes. Do not force every activity every day or
accelerate hunger merely to keep him busy. Travel distances and regrowth should
support revisiting known places without turning the world into a compact demo.

## Suggested playable rhythm, not a forced schedule

Morning: assess needs and recall known food/water; choose today's useful goal.
Day: forage or explore a nearby unknown area, then collect/process materials if
needs allow. Evening: return to shelter, use an available improvement and rest.
New observations or a user request may change the plan. Resource knowledge can
be stale, so arrival must verify the remembered opportunity still exists.

## Shared ticket completion gates

Each ticket needs source provenance, isolated tests, real compiled-player
evidence, scoped save/reload checks and a clear integration dependency. Retain
one useful screenshot/clip and truthful short narrative. A tracker ticket is
not implementation; an implemented component is not a released game.
