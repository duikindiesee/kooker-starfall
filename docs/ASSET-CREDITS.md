## R05 generated derivatives of PH01

The original PH01 FBX and six downloaded textures remain byte-for-byte unchanged at the hashes in its provenance manifest. The **R05 hybrid experiment is a derivative**, not the unmodified imported model shown in preserved Round 03. Its setup extracts **five source rosette groups, A–E, with 21 leaf components each** from the pinned PH01 FBX. Extraction retains the source polygon-corner UVs and normals, triangulates source faces and converts coordinates/winding around each measured root pivot. Those extracted meshes are then selected, rotated, scaled and placed on a **modified procedural branch architecture**.

The R05 material setup applies a modest blue-green leaf tint to the original albedo. Bark uses source maps sampled inside selected interior rectangles of the PH01 UV atlas; the complete atlas is not treated as a seamless bark tile. These placement, tint and mapping choices are modifications in the generated inspection assets even though the downloaded files are unchanged. The [PH01 manifest](../Assets/CityLife/Art/PolyHaven/QuiverTree01/provenance.json) records this derivative use separately from the file inventory.

The source also contains separate closed components near leaf bases. Their identity and suitability remain **unverified**. The hybrid setup keeps them only in the separate source diagnostics **18–21**, reached in R07; they are **not included in the hybrid trees**. Component extraction does not certify the source's Latin species or convert these ambiguous pieces into identified plant anatomy.

R05 failed shader compilation after one capture. R07 later completed all **21 captures** and was independently **rejected: botanical 6.00, art 4.75, overall 4.75/10**. R09 retains PH01 and was also rejected after 21 reviewed captures: **botanical 6.375, art 5.50, overall 5.50/10**. The separate R06 local WIP preview was baked and launched. The user has since approved its tree as the [retained visual reference](TREE-BASELINE.md); that approval does not establish full runtime acceptance or larger-world integration. The [R09 hybrid numerical report](../evidence/verified/kokerboom-round-09-hybrid-validation.json) separately passes 380 assertions, independent regeneration, nine closed wood topology samples and unchanged source inputs. This applies to its recorded source and does not inherit R04's procedural pass or establish visual/runtime suitability. The [R09 PH01 source-identity diagnostic](../evidence/milestones/kokerboom/round-09/ph01-source-identity.json) matches position/normal/UV for all 94,176 extracted corners; imported tangent preservation remains a separate controlled comparison. See the [preserved critique history](KOKERBOOM-CRITIQUE.md).

The editor-only [PH01 imported-tuple candidate](../Assets/CityLife/Editor/PH01ImportedTupleCandidate.cs) reads the actual Unity-imported position/normal/UV/tangent4 tuples from the unchanged source. Its comparison retains the existing extractor triangulation, with optional exact-tuple deduplication that preserves attribute seams. An original imported triangle subset is included only when its triangle-count and mapped-corner-coverage checks agree. No normals/UVs are flipped to hide a tangent difference, copied tangents are not recalculated, and unmatched or materially ambiguous mappings fail explicitly. [R10's 12 scoped captures and actual mapping report](../evidence/milestones/kokerboom/round-10/metrics.json) passed technical checks with zero unmatched/ambiguous corners and no deduplication. The [independent review](KOKERBOOM-CRITIQUE.md#round-10-scoped-ph01-tangent-comparison-no-meaningful-visible-gain) found no meaningful visible gain. This candidate remains outside the family generator and is not claimed as a visual fix.

## PH02 open crown candidate

The original PH02 FBX and four downloaded textures remain unchanged. [PH02CrownCandidate.cs](../Assets/CityLife/Editor/PH02CrownCandidate.cs) is an editor-only derivative extractor for a scoped original/cut/attachment comparison. It decodes **41,073 control vertices and 82,074 triangles** from the pinned source, preserving **ByVertice/IndexToDirect normals** and **ByPolygonVertex/IndexToDirect UVs**. Output is metres/Y-up with reflected winding; both full and cut meshes retain the original source origin. Source diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps are assigned with white tint. The mask is not assumed to be opacity without evidence.

The explicit trial plane is **Y=0.65 m** in original converted coordinates. The measured cut intersects 69 source triangles and emits 71,207 triangles with one open 69-segment stem loop, approximately 0.148×0.150 m across. Corner attributes are preserved; new boundary positions/UVs are interpolated and interpolated normals normalized. The cut is not capped. Geometry above a single narrow section is not proof that every hanging leaf is intact, so the parameter and visual acceptance remain explicit. A separate mid-gray open tapered support tests attachment by translation at the measured boundary centre with 0.04 m overlap; it does not weld, cap, resize or certify the crown.

Eight candidate-only views were captured in [R08](../evidence/milestones/kokerboom/round-08/metrics.json): original neutral/cosmic, cut front/side/under/above, and attachment overview/close-up. Technical capture checks passed with zero errors and one warning. The [independent scoped review](KOKERBOOM-CRITIQUE.md#round-08-scoped-ph02-crown-review) assigns natural rosette **8.0/10 only**, identifies no obvious new blade loss, and leaves attachment, palette and full-family acceptance unresolved. Standalone compilation and CPU source-mapping/clipping checks passed before integration. No previous procedural 349-assertion result or full-family score applies. [The PH02 manifest](../Assets/CityLife/Art/PolyHaven/QuiverTree02/provenance.json) records this derivative separately from unchanged downloaded files.

A further editor-only [fitted-support candidate](../Assets/CityLife/Editor/PH02FittedSupportCandidate.cs) keeps that crown unchanged and constructs a separate open support from the actual irregular cut rim. Top position/normal/UV/tangent attributes are retained; below the rim, source-atlas gradients are extrapolated while vertex red blends toward a pale support material over the chosen 0.25 m transition. Atlas extrapolation may cross source texture islands and requires textured review. [The custom support shader](../Assets/CityLife/Shaders/PH02FittedSupport.shader) uses the same source diffuse/normal/roughness bindings at the top. This is new derivative geometry/material mapping, not part of the downloaded PH02 model or a whole-tree weld. R12 failed the harness's boundary selector before images; [R13 completed twelve views and exact actual rim checks](../evidence/milestones/kokerboom/round-13/metrics.json). The [independent scoped review](KOKERBOOM-CRITIQUE.md#round-13-scoped-fitted-attachment-improved) accepts it as an improved attachment candidate, while lower-tube/branch overlap, detail loss and cosmic palette remain unresolved. No full-family score or world acceptance follows.

The optional `-PH02Fitted -PH02ImportedTuples` comparison uses [PH02ImportedCrownCandidate.cs](../Assets/CityLife/Editor/PH02ImportedCrownCandidate.cs). It maps whole source triangles and winding to actual Unity-imported P/N/UV/tangent tuples, interpolates new cut attributes, and deduplicates only when all twelve IEEE float bit patterns agree. It never recalculates copied normals/tangents. The fitter owns a clone and records caller/clone hashes; source identity is established by the separate adapter report. The original raw-source path remains the default. [R15's actual report and twelve captures](../evidence/milestones/kokerboom/round-15/metrics.json) verify all 82,074 source triangles mapped, 71,207 crown triangles retained, and 213,621 corners reduced to 43,200 vertices with matching expanded tuple/Mesh hashes. Exact reversed rim checks pass and R13 cameras are retained. No appearance improvement, family acceptance or FPS benefit is inferred. Source files and their licence/hash inventory remain unchanged.

## PH02 fitted-crown family R16

The [PH02 family component](../Assets/CityLife/Editor/PH02FamilyComponent.cs) combines that crown and fitted support into one material mesh, rigidly centred/aligned at the measured lower support frame. It preserves separate crown/support index spans and complete attributes. Vertex red carries source-to-support material blend; green is an explicitly documented geometric foliage-tint heuristic, not a publisher semantic mask. The new family inspection profile uses PH01 mapped bark on procedural wood and a PH02 compound material; tint defaults to zero, and its albedo diagnostic preserves the compound blend. Component validation does not establish the changed `v2-preview` family's numeric, visual or runtime acceptance. The [completed R16 manifest](../evidence/milestones/kokerboom/round-16/metrics.json) preserves 21 actual views with tint 0, zero errors and one warning. The component keeps native metric scale while its family placement applies seeded rotation and modest per-rosette scale variation; it is not an unmodified original-model instance. The independent full-family [R16 review](KOKERBOOM-CRITIQUE.md#round-16-ph02-fitted-crown-family-rejected) rejects the result at botanical 6.75, art 5.75 and overall 5.75. Fuller PH02 rosettes are retained; support/wood contrast, bend shading, ground termination, bark and olive/cool-foliage identity remain unresolved. These derivative-use records do not imply publisher endorsement, species certification, world integration or inclusion of the R16 family in the preserved R06 player package.
