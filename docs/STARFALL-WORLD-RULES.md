# Starfall world rule: knowledge is progression

Durable design rule, recorded from the user's direction on 13 September 2026. This does not expand the current combined-world build.

A new inhabitant begins cognitively naive. Minimal embodied abilities permit noticing discomfort, moving, looking, resting, asking and attempting permitted simple actions. They do not imply knowledge of local plants, animals, locations, recipes, hazards or defenses. A model's pretrained knowledge is not inhabitant memory.

Perception, cautious permitted experiments, observed outcomes, communication and verified death lessons create provenance-tracked beliefs. Each belief must identify world/generation, inhabitant, subject/form, assertion, source events, confidence and last observation. Confidence may change; later evidence may contradict a belief. Preserve earlier observations and append corrections rather than rewriting history. Communication is attributed testimony until verified. Unobserved stock, hidden hazards and unexplored locations are not revealed through dialogue or a planner snapshot.

The first purple-red berry is safely learnable through a signed tutorial lesson. It is not a poisonous-plant guessing game. Unknown food is not automatically consumed even during hunger. Current food code uses scoped lesson/outcome flags; a general confidence and correction system remains roadmap work.

Unity owns all physical and survival consequences, perception, permissions, navigation, food safety, injury, inventory, plants/populations and saves. Optional inference proposes bounded goals or dialogue, never grants knowledge or changes those states. The player must see a legible cue, an action or avoidance opportunity, and the observed outcome.

Death belongs to a persistent world. Terrain, weather clock, structures, ecology/depletion and shared history do not reset or rewind. The same inhabitant returns at the refuge in a new body incarnation with baseline physiology. Carried items enter an owned recovery container rather than being silently destroyed or duplicated. Memory remains scoped to the same world/inhabitant. New worlds, reset generations and new inhabitants do not inherit acquired facts.

Each death records a verified cause and contributing conditions immutably. At most one new lesson may be derived from an existing mechanic the inhabitant has not learned. It may refer only to already known items, observed places or a safe authored mechanic; it cannot reveal unknown plants, hidden springs or predator locations. A death with no eligible lesson gives no invented insight. Repeated deaths do not farm knowledge or rewind scarcity.

Future poisonous plants and predators may injure or kill, but require readable warnings, avoidance/retreat, deterministic Unity authority, bounded animal behaviour, fair counterplay and save-scoped memory. Refuge defenses may later include fire, noise, barriers and sharpened stakes. None is innate knowledge. Nonlethal deterrence precedes defensive crafting/combat; defenses need learnable prerequisites and limitations.

See [staged roadmap](STARFALL-SURVIVAL-ROADMAP.md), [food adapter](FOOD-INTEGRATION.md) and [memory contract](STARFALL-MEMORY.md). Concept art is never gameplay proof, model assertions are never observed facts, and automated checks are never human acceptance.
